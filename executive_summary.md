## The Opportunity

**Spribe** is a Georgian-founded B2B game provider that created **Aviator** — the world's dominant Crash game — and now commands **92% market share** of the fastest-growing segment in online gambling.

## Why Crash Games Win

1. **Simplicity** — One button, one decision. No rules to learn. 3-second rounds.
2. **Social proof** — Real-time multiplayer with visible bets = viral loop
3. **Mobile-native** — Perfect for mobile-first markets (India, Africa)
4. **Lower acquisition cost** — Player LTV/CAC ratio 2-3x better than slots
5. **Gen Z appeal** — Gamification mechanics resonate with younger players

## Market Position

```
Online Gambling TAM: ~$100B GGR (2024)
  └─ Online Casino: ~$35B (35%)
       └─ Crash Games: ~$6-8B (growing to $12-15B by 2027)
            └─ Spribe/Aviator: ~$6-7B (92% share)
```

**Crash is the fastest-growing casino sub-segment** at 25-30% CAGR vs. 8-10% for slots.

## Revenue Model

```
$14B wager/month × 3% margin (97% RTP) = ~$420M GGR/month
$420-550M GGR × 8% avg take rate = ~$34-44M/month Spribe revenue
Annualized: $400-528M/year
```

EBITDA margin estimated at **$300-400M (60-80%)**. Evolution Gaming's EBITDA margin is 66% — comparable, but Spribe achieves this with 350 people vs. Evolution's 20,000+ (14x revenue/employee advantage).

## Risk Factors

1. **Single-product concentration** — Aviator = ~90%+ of revenue
2. **US market blocked** — Missing world's largest gambling market
3. **Regulatory risk** — Grey market dependence (India, Africa)
4. **Competition emerging** — Pragmatic Play (Spaceman), SmartSoft (JetX)
5. **Genre risk** — Will Crash games remain popular long-term?

## Marketing Machine

- **UFC**: $40M/3yr global sponsorship (700M+ viewers)
- **AC Milan**: European + LatAm brand reach
- **Cricket leagues**: IPL, SA20 → India, South Africa
- **WWE**: Entertainment crossover
- **Affiliates**: Gypsy Partners, AFFPRO, YouTube/Twitch streamers

## Investment Thesis

**Bull case ($5B+):**
- Brazil regulation adds $50-100M/yr revenue
- Product diversification (new games, new verticals)
- Margin expansion with scale
- IPO at Evolution-like multiples

**Bear case ($1.5B):**
- Crash fatigue / genre decline
- Regulatory crackdowns in grey markets
- Competitor closes gap
- Key market tax changes (India GST)

## Bottom Line

Spribe is a **$500M revenue, 60-80% EBITDA margin, category-defining B2B software company** growing 20-30% annually in the fastest-growing segment of a $100B+ market. At a **$1.5-2B private valuation** (3-4x P/S), it trades at a modest discount to Evolution Gaming (4.9x P/S, $13.4B MCap). With **380M registered players, EUR 160B annual wagers, and 350K bets/minute**, Spribe has built network effects that are difficult to replicate. A successful IPO could see **$3-5B valuation** within 2-3 years if growth sustains.

**Key upside catalyst:** Brazil ($7B market, newly regulated) + UAE (historic market opening 2025-26).
