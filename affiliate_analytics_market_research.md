### 1.4 RevShare Standard Rates

| Tier | RevShare Range | Notes |
|------|---------------|-------|
| Standard programs | 20-35% | Entry-level affiliates |
| Quality programs | 35-50% | Most common for active affiliates |
| Premium/VIP | 50-60% | High-volume partners, 7Stars Partners up to 60% |
| Maximum offers | Up to 80% | Kingfin (exceptional, likely time-limited) |
| **Industry standard** | **30-50%** | Most programs fall here |

**Important mechanics:**
- RevShare calculated on Net Gaming Revenue (NGR) after bonuses, chargebacks, fraud clawbacks, taxes
- Typical contract duration: up to 3 years
- Negative carryover: most programs carry negative balances forward
- Payment: monthly (sometimes weekly)

## KEY TAKEAWAYS

1. **Affiliate market is consolidating** — Better Collective ($387M revenue) dominates, XLMedia exited, mid-tier affiliates shrinking
2. **CPA disparity is huge** — US: $200-400, Africa: $5-30 per FTD. This creates arbitrage opportunities
3. **RevShare standard is 30-50%** — Premium partners can negotiate 50-60%
4. **No crash-game-specific affiliate infrastructure exists** — all runs through general casino programs
5. **Crash game content is MASSIVE in Tier 2-3** — Aviator drives billions of TikTok views, mainly India/Africa/Brazil
6. **Analytics market is fragmented** — H2 Gambling Capital and Eilers & Krejcik are enterprise-only, no affordable mid-market analytics product exists
7. **Compliance tooling is expensive** — GeoComply/Sumsub dominate, but pricing is opaque and enterprise-focused
8. **Payment orchestration market: $1.42B** — Africa and India are the fastest-growing payment regions for iGaming
9. **UPI dominates India** — 90% of bettors use it, near-zero transaction costs
10. **Africa mobile money (M-Pesa) is king** — East Africa runs on M-Pesa; West Africa on Flutterwave/Paystack
